The Philippine economy’s recent performance shows a strong bounce‑back. Even amid worldwide tensions, the nation’s growth has stayed on track, thanks largely to lively domestic spending and money flowing in from Filipinos abroad. The government’s focus has turned to building up infrastructure, putting forward several projects that aim to tighten connections and boost development in less‑served areas. Another bright spot is the digital sector – e‑commerce and fintech are taking off fast, driving new gains. Still, the country grapples with stubborn problems like rising prices and unequal earnings, issues that demand ongoing policy action.
